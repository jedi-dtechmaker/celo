# Vorra

**Autonomous AI Procurement & Cross-Border Commerce Platform Built on Celo**

Version: 1.0

Status: Product Definition

---

# Executive Summary

Vorra is an AI-powered B2B procurement platform that enables African businesses to discover suppliers, negotiate purchases, execute secure escrow payments, and manage cross-border trade using Celo stablecoins.

Unlike traditional marketplaces where buyers manually browse products and negotiate with suppliers, Vorra introduces intelligent AI agents capable of understanding procurement requests in natural language, comparing suppliers, evaluating trust, assisting negotiations, and automating purchases within user-defined rules.

The platform combines a familiar marketplace experience with autonomous AI assistance and programmable payments on Celo.

---

# Vision

To become Africa's AI operating system for commerce.

---

# Mission

Simplify cross-border trade in Africa by enabling businesses to purchase goods through intelligent AI agents while settling transactions securely using blockchain-based escrow.

---

# The Problem

Cross-border procurement in Africa remains fragmented.

Businesses often spend days or weeks:

* Searching suppliers
* Comparing prices
* Verifying legitimacy
* Negotiating prices
* Managing payments
* Coordinating logistics
* Handling disputes

Payments are often slow, expensive, and risky.

Trust remains one of the largest barriers to trade.

# Primary Use Case

A Nigerian food manufacturer requires 500kg of export-quality maize.

Instead of contacting multiple suppliers manually, they open Vorra and ask:

> Find 500kg of Grade A maize from Ghana under 450 cUSD.

Vorra searches the marketplace, compares suppliers, evaluates trust scores, estimates delivery, recommends the best option, creates an escrow agreement, and prepares payment.
